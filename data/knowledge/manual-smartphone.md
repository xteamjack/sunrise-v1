# Sunrise Smartphone: User Manual

Thank you for buying a Sunrise smartphone. This short guide covers setup, common issues, and care.

## Setup
1. Charge the phone before the first use with the supplied cable and adapter.
2. Insert the SIM card and, if needed, a memory card using the tray tool in the box.
3. Switch on and follow the steps to choose language, network, and screen lock.
4. Sign in to your account, connect to Wi-Fi, and install pending updates.
5. Restore your contacts and apps from a backup if you have one.

## Common issues
- Will not switch on: charge for fifteen minutes, then press and hold the power button.
- Battery drains fast: lower brightness, close background apps, and turn off features you do not use.
- No network: check the SIM is seated, toggle flight mode, or restart the phone.
- Storage full: remove unused apps, clear the cache, and move photos to cloud or a card.
- Slow or stuck: restart the phone. If it stays slow, back up and reset from settings.

## Care
- Use a case and screen guard to reduce damage from drops.
- Keep the phone away from water unless your model is rated for it.
- Clean the screen with a soft dry cloth.
- Avoid charging in very hot places and use a good quality charger.

## Warranty and support
Standard warranty covers manufacturing defects. Physical or liquid damage is not covered and is handled as a dispute. For order status, returns, or a simple warranty question, contact Sunrise support with your order number.
