# Sunrise Speaker: User Manual

Thank you for buying a Sunrise speaker. This short guide covers setup, common issues, and care.

## Setup
1. Charge the speaker fully before the first use, or connect it to power for mains models.
2. Switch it on and put it into pairing mode by holding the Bluetooth button until the light blinks.
3. On your phone or laptop, open Bluetooth settings and select the Sunrise speaker.
4. Play audio to confirm sound and set a comfortable volume.
5. For wired use, connect the audio cable instead of pairing.

## Common issues
- Will not pair: switch Bluetooth off and on, remove the old pairing, and try again.
- No sound: check the volume on both the speaker and the device, and confirm the right output is selected.
- Distorted sound: lower the volume and move away from other wireless devices.
- Keeps disconnecting: stay within range and keep the speaker charged.
- Will not switch on: charge for fifteen minutes and try again.

## Care
- Keep the speaker away from water unless your model is rated for it.
- Wipe it with a soft dry cloth.
- Store it in a cool, dry place.
- Charge with a good quality cable and avoid leaving it fully drained.

## Warranty and support
Standard warranty covers manufacturing defects. Physical or liquid damage is not covered and is handled as a dispute. For order status, returns, or a simple warranty question, contact Sunrise support with your order number.
