# Sunrise Camera: User Manual

Thank you for buying a Sunrise camera. This short guide covers setup, common issues, and care.

## Setup
1. Charge the battery fully and insert it, along with a memory card, before the first use.
2. Switch on and follow the steps to set date, time, and language.
3. Choose a basic auto mode for your first photos.
4. To transfer photos, connect the camera to a computer with the cable, or remove the card and use a card reader.
5. Install any firmware update offered for your model.

## Common issues
- Will not switch on: charge the battery fully and check that it is seated correctly.
- Cannot take a photo: check that a memory card is inserted and not full or locked.
- Blurry photos: hold the camera steady, clean the lens, and let it focus before you press fully.
- Card error: format the card in the camera after backing up your photos.
- Flash not firing: turn the flash on in the menu and let it charge between shots.

## Care
- Clean the lens with a soft lens cloth only. Do not use tissue or strong cleaners.
- Keep the camera in a padded bag, away from dust and water.
- Remove the battery if you will not use the camera for a long time.
- Avoid very hot or damp places.

## Warranty and support
Standard warranty covers manufacturing defects. Physical or liquid damage is not covered and is handled as a dispute. For order status, returns, or a simple warranty question, contact Sunrise support with your order number.
