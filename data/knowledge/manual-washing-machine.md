# Sunrise Washing Machine: User Manual

Thank you for buying a Sunrise washing machine. This short guide covers setup, common issues, and care.

## Setup
1. Place the machine on a firm, level floor. Remove the transport bolts at the back before the first wash.
2. Connect the inlet hose to a tap and the drain hose to an outlet. Check for leaks.
3. Connect to a stable, earthed power point.
4. Load clothes without overfilling, add the right amount of detergent, and close the door.
5. Select a wash program and press start.

## Common issues
- Will not start: check the power, that the door is shut, and that the tap is open.
- Not draining: clear the drain filter and check the drain hose for a bend or block.
- Too much vibration: level the feet and confirm the transport bolts were removed.
- Clothes still wet: choose a higher spin speed and do not overload.
- Bad smell: run an empty hot wash now and then, and leave the door open to dry.

## Care
- Clean the drum, drawer, and filter regularly.
- Use the correct detergent and the right quantity for the load.
- Wipe the door seal after each wash.
- Close the tap when the machine is not in use.

## Warranty and support
Standard warranty covers manufacturing defects. Physical or liquid damage is not covered and is handled as a dispute. For order status, returns, or a simple warranty question, contact Sunrise support with your order number.
