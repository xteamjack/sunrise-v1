# Sunrise Television: User Manual

Thank you for buying a Sunrise television. This short guide covers setup, common issues, and care.

## Setup
1. Place the television on a firm, flat surface or fix the wall mount to a solid wall using the bracket in the box.
2. Connect the power cable to a stable power point. Use a voltage stabiliser if your area has voltage swings.
3. Connect your set top box, gaming console, or streaming stick to an HDMI port.
4. Insert the batteries into the remote, switch on, and follow the on screen steps to select language, network, and channels.
5. Sign in to your streaming apps if your model supports them.

## Common issues
- No picture: check that the correct HDMI or source is selected using the Source button.
- No sound: check the volume, the mute setting, and whether an external speaker is connected.
- Remote not working: replace the batteries and clear the line of sight to the screen.
- App not loading: check the network connection and restart the television from settings.
- Lines or flicker on screen: switch off, wait one minute, and switch on again. If it stays, contact support.

## Care
- Clean the screen with a soft dry cloth. Do not spray liquid directly on the panel.
- Keep vents free so heat can escape. Do not block the back of the set.
- Switch off at the mains during long absences and storms.

## Warranty and support
Standard warranty covers manufacturing defects. Physical or liquid damage is not covered and is handled as a dispute. For status, returns, or a simple warranty question, contact Sunrise support with your order number.
