# Sunrise Refrigerator: User Manual

Thank you for buying a Sunrise refrigerator. This short guide covers setup, common issues, and care.

## Setup
1. Stand the refrigerator upright and let it rest for at least six hours before switching on, especially after transport.
2. Place it on a firm, level floor with a small gap around the back and sides for airflow.
3. Connect to a stable power point. Use a voltage stabiliser if your area has voltage swings.
4. Set the cooling level to a medium setting first, then adjust to taste.
5. Wait a few hours for it to cool before loading food.

## Common issues
- Not cooling: check the power, the temperature setting, and that the door seals shut.
- Water collecting inside: clear the small drain hole at the back of the fridge section.
- Too much frost: avoid keeping the door open long, and check the door gasket.
- Noise or vibration: level the feet and keep a gap from the wall.
- Bad smell: clean the shelves and keep an open box of baking soda inside.

## Care
- Clean the inside with a soft cloth and mild soap. Do not use harsh cleaners.
- Defrost as advised for your model.
- Keep the door gasket clean so it seals well.
- Do not overload the shelves, so air can move.

## Warranty and support
Standard warranty covers manufacturing defects. Physical or liquid damage is not covered and is handled as a dispute. For order status, returns, or a simple warranty question, contact Sunrise support with your order number.
