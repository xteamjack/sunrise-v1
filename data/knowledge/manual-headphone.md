# Sunrise Headphone: User Manual

Thank you for buying a Sunrise headphone. This short guide covers setup, common issues, and care.

## Setup
1. Charge the headphone fully before the first use.
2. Switch it on and put it into pairing mode by holding the power button until the light blinks.
3. On your phone or laptop, open Bluetooth settings and select the Sunrise device.
4. Once paired, play audio to confirm sound in both sides.
5. For wired use, connect the cable to the audio port instead of pairing.

## Common issues
- Will not pair: switch Bluetooth off and on, remove the old pairing, and try again.
- Sound only on one side: check the balance setting and make sure both sides are charged.
- Low volume or muffled sound: clean the mesh, and check the volume on both the device and the headphone.
- Keeps disconnecting: stay within range and away from other wireless devices.
- Microphone not heard on calls: allow microphone access in the app and select the headphone as the input.

## Care
- Store the headphone in its case when not in use.
- Wipe the ear pads with a soft dry cloth.
- Keep it away from water unless your model is rated for sweat or rain.
- Charge with a good quality cable and avoid leaving it fully drained.

## Warranty and support
Standard warranty covers manufacturing defects. Physical or liquid damage is not covered and is handled as a dispute. For order status, returns, or a simple warranty question, contact Sunrise support with your order number.
