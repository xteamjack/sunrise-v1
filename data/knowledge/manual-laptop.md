# Sunrise Laptop: User Manual

Thank you for buying a Sunrise laptop. This short guide covers setup, common issues, and care.

## Setup
1. Charge the laptop fully before the first use using the supplied adapter.
2. Press the power button and follow the on screen steps to choose language, region, and network.
3. Create your user account and set a password.
4. Connect to your Wi-Fi network and install any pending updates.
5. Sign in to your work or personal accounts and restore your files.

## Common issues
- Will not switch on: connect the charger, wait ten minutes, then press and hold the power button for ten seconds.
- Battery drains fast: lower the screen brightness, close unused apps, and check for a background update.
- Slow performance: restart the laptop, remove startup apps you do not need, and keep some free storage.
- Wi-Fi not connecting: switch flight mode on and off, or forget the network and join again.
- Overheating: use it on a hard flat surface so the vents stay clear.

## Care
- Clean the screen and keyboard with a soft, slightly damp cloth. Do not use strong cleaners.
- Carry the laptop in a padded bag and avoid pressure on the lid.
- Keep liquids away from the keyboard.
- Charge in a cool, dry place and avoid full discharge often.

## Warranty and support
Standard warranty covers manufacturing defects. Physical or liquid damage is not covered and is handled as a dispute. For order status, returns, or a simple warranty question, contact Sunrise support with your order number.
